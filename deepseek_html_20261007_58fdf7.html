Every corner has a story. We listen.

Feeling the Streets is a grassroots charity that walks alongside people experiencing homelessness — providing meals, warmth, and a path to stability. Real people. Real streets. Real change.

12,400+ meals served

Downtown outreach · Today

More than a meal

We meet immediate needs while building long-term relationships and solutions.

Street kitchen

Hot, nutritious meals served 7 nights a week from our mobile kitchen — no questions asked.

Hygiene & care

Access to showers, clean clothes, and hygiene kits. Dignity is a right, not a privilege.

Housing navigation

Personal caseworkers help secure IDs, benefits, and permanent supportive housing.

Community

Weekly support circles and art therapy — because connection heals.
